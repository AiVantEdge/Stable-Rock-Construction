If you've been putting off impact windows because of the cost, there's a state program worth knowing about: **My Safe Florida Home (MSFH)**. It helps eligible homeowners pay for the upgrades that harden a house against hurricanes — and those same upgrades usually earn you an insurance discount on top.

## What the program offers

MSFH provides **matching grants toward wind-mitigation improvements** — commonly up to **$10,000**, typically on a **$2-for-$1 match** (the state contributes two dollars for every dollar you put in). Lower-income homeowners may qualify for a grant with no match required. Eligible upgrades include **impact-rated windows and doors**, reinforced garage doors, and roof-to-wall connections.

## Who generally qualifies

Eligibility rules change between funding cycles, but the usual requirements are:

- The home is your **homesteaded primary residence**
- It's a **site-built, single-family home**
- It was built **before a recent code cutoff** (older homes are the target)
- The home's **insured value is under a set cap**
- You complete a **free wind-mitigation inspection first**

## The one step people get wrong

**Do not start the work before your grant is approved.** The process is: apply → get the free wind-mitigation inspection → receive approval → *then* do the installation with an approved contractor. Work started before approval usually won't be reimbursed. Funding also runs out fast each cycle, so timing matters.

## How this stacks with insurance

The grant helps with the up-front cost; the **wind-mitigation credit** helps every year after. Remember that insurers generally require **full opening protection** (all windows and doors) to grant the credit — a partial job usually won't qualify.
